    type: UUID
    required: false

    relationType: promotes

  - name: status
    type: string

    required: true
    description: When the campaign starts.
  - name: endsAt
    type: datetime
    required: true
    description: When the campaign ends.

## Overview

Campaigns are owned by the Campaign Service, which sends their offers to customers as [[event|campaign-offer-sent]]. A campaign often promotes a promotion that the Shopping domain applies at checkout.
