## Overview

The **Returns** subdomain is part of the [[domain|fulfilment]] domain and runs the reverse logistics journey through the [[system|returns-system]].

1. A customer sends [[command|request-return]] and the [[service|returns-api]] publishes [[event|return-requested]].
2. The [[service|returns-processor]] checks the returns policy and publishes [[event|return-approved]].
3. When the parcel reaches the warehouse it publishes [[event|return-received]] — Payments refunds the customer and Inventory restocks the items.
