## Overview

The **Loyalty System** turns orders into rewards. The [[service|loyalty-service]] consumes [[event|order-completed]] and [[event|order-cancelled]] from the [[system|order-management-system]], updates balances in the [[container|loyalty-database]], and publishes points events for the rest of the business.

## What's inside

| Component | Type | Responsibility |
|-----------|------|----------------|
| [[service\|loyalty-service]] | Service | Awards and reverses loyalty points and maintains balances. |
| [[container\|loyalty-database]] | Data store | Points ledger and customer balances. |

## Messages this system sends

- [[event\|loyalty-points-earned]] — points were awarded for a completed order.
- [[event\|loyalty-points-reversed]] — points were removed after an order was cancelled.
