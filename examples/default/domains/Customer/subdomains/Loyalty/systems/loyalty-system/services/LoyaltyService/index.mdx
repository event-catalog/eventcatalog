## Overview

The **Loyalty Service** consumes [[event|order-completed]] and [[event|order-cancelled]] from the Ordering domain. It records points in the [[container|loyalty-database]] and publishes [[event|loyalty-points-earned]] or [[event|loyalty-points-reversed]] so that promotions and customer notifications can react.

### Responsibilities

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Earning | Awards points on [[event\|order-completed]] and emits [[event\|loyalty-points-earned]]. |
| Reversal | Removes points on [[event\|order-cancelled]] and emits [[event\|loyalty-points-reversed]]. |
| Persistence | Reads from and writes to the [[container\|loyalty-database]]. |
