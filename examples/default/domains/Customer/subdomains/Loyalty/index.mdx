## Overview

The **Loyalty** subdomain is part of the [[domain|customer]] domain. It listens to the order lifecycle from the Ordering domain and turns completed orders into loyalty points that customers can redeem on future purchases.

| Trigger | Outcome |
|---------|---------|
| [[event\|order-completed]] | Points are awarded and [[event\|loyalty-points-earned]] is published. |
| [[event\|order-cancelled]] | Previously awarded points are removed and [[event\|loyalty-points-reversed]] is published. |
