### What is this?

The **Loyalty Database** is the authoritative store for loyalty points. The [[service|loyalty-service]] appends a ledger entry for every award or reversal and keeps a running balance per customer.

### What does it store?

- **Points ledger** — one row per award or reversal, linked to the order that caused it.
- **Balances** — current points balance and tier per customer.
