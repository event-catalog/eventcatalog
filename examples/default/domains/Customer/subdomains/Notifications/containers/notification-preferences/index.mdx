### What is this?

The **Notification Preferences** store records how each customer wants to be contacted. The [[service|notification-service]] reads it before every send so customers only hear from us on channels they have opted into.

### What does it store?

- **Channels** — email address, phone number and push tokens, with per-channel opt-in.
- **Topics** — which notification types (orders, delivery, loyalty, reviews) the customer wants.
- **Quiet hours** — times when non-urgent notifications are held back.
