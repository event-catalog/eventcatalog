## Overview

The **Customer Notifications** subdomain is part of the [[domain|customer]] domain. It has no systems of its own — a single [[service|notification-service]] consumes events from across the business and notifies customers according to their [[container|notification-preferences]].

| Source domain | Events |
|---------------|--------|
| Ordering | [[event\|order-confirmed]] |
| Fulfilment | [[event\|shipment-delivered]], [[event\|return-approved]] |
| Payments | [[event\|payment-failed]], [[event\|refund-processed]] |
| Reviews | [[event\|review-published]] |
| Customer (Loyalty) | [[event\|loyalty-points-earned]] |
