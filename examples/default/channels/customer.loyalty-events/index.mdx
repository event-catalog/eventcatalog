## Overview

The [[service|loyalty-service]] publishes [[event|loyalty-points-earned]] and [[event|loyalty-points-reversed]] to this topic as customers earn and lose points.

| Consumer | Domain | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| [[service\|promotion-service]] | Shopping | Offers promotions based on a customer's points. |
| [[service\|notification-service]] | Customer (Notifications) | Tells the customer about the points they've earned. |
