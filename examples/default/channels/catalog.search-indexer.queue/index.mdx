## Overview

The [[service|search-indexer]] consumes product events from this queue and applies them to the search index. The queue is the final channel in the product-event routing chain.

Messages the indexer fails to process five times are moved to the [[channel|catalog.search-indexer.dlq]] dead letter queue.
