## Overview

The Returns System publishes the lifecycle of a return to this topic. The [[service|returns-api]] publishes [[event|return-requested]], and the [[service|returns-processor]] publishes [[event|return-approved]] and [[event|return-received]].

| Consumer | Domain | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| [[service\|returns-processor]] | Fulfilment (Returns) | Reviews new return requests. |
| [[service\|payment-worker]] | Payments | Refunds the customer once the items are received. |
| [[service\|inventory-service]] | Fulfilment | Restocks returned items. |
| [[service\|notification-service]] | Customer (Notifications) | Tells the customer their return is approved. |
