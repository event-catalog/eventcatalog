## Overview

The [[service|order-service]] publishes the order lifecycle to this topic: [[event|order-created]], [[event|order-confirmed]], [[event|order-completed]] and [[event|order-cancelled]]. Other domains subscribe to it rather than calling the Ordering domain directly:

| Consumer | Domain | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| [[service\|warehouse-service]] | Fulfilment | Starts picking once an order completes. |
| [[service\|loyalty-service]] | Customer (Loyalty) | Awards points for completed orders and reverses them for cancelled ones. |
| [[service\|notification-service]] | Customer (Notifications) | Tells the customer their order is confirmed. |
