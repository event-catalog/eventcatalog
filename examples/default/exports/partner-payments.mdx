Welcome! This guide covers everything you need to take payment for an Acme order and react to what happens afterwards.

## How it works

1. Your app checks out a cart through the [[service|checkout-api]].
2. We authorise the payment and screen it for fraud.
3. You receive [[event|payment-succeeded]] or [[event|payment-failed]], then [[event|refund-processed]] if the order is refunded later.

## Verifying events

Each webhook request carries an `Acme-Signature` header. Check it against your signing secret before you trust the payload.
